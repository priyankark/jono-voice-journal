# Privacy Policy for Jono

**Last Updated: February 2026**

## Overview

Jono ("we", "our", or "the app") is a voice-first journaling application designed with privacy as a core principle. This Privacy Policy explains how Jono handles your information.

## Our Privacy Commitment

**Your data stays on your device. Always.**

Jono is built from the ground up to be a completely local, privacy-first application. We do not collect, transmit, or store any of your personal data on external servers.

## Data Collection

### What We Do NOT Collect

- **No personal information**: We do not collect your name, email, phone number, or any identifying information
- **No account required**: Jono works without sign-up or registration
- **No voice data transmitted**: Your voice recordings are never uploaded to external servers
- **No analytics or tracking**: We do not track your usage patterns, behaviors, or interactions
- **No location data**: We do not access or store your location
- **No third-party data sharing**: We do not share any information with third parties because we don't collect any

### What Stays on Your Device

All of the following data is stored locally on your device only:

- Voice recordings
- Transcribed text from your recordings
- Journal entries and conversations
- Generated content (poems, letters, essays, etc.)
- Personal insights and analysis
- Tags and categories
- App preferences and settings

## Voice Processing

### Speech Recognition

Jono uses Apple's on-device speech recognition technology (Apple Intelligence) to transcribe your voice recordings. This processing:

- Happens entirely on your iPhone
- Does not send your voice data to any external servers
- Does not require an internet connection for basic transcription
- Is controlled by Apple's privacy standards

### Voice Recording

When you record voice notes:

- Audio files are stored only in the app's local storage
- Recordings can be deleted at any time by you
- No backups are made to external services
- Recordings are not accessible by other apps

## Data Storage

### Local Storage Only

All your data is stored using your device's secure local storage. This means:

- Data is protected by your device's security (Face ID, Touch ID, passcode)
- Data is encrypted by iOS at the device level
- Only you can access your journal entries
- Data does not sync to iCloud or any cloud service (unless you choose to back up your entire device)

### Data Deletion

You have complete control over your data:

- Delete individual entries at any time from within the app
- Delete all recordings and transcriptions
- Uninstalling Jono removes all associated data permanently

## Children's Privacy

Jono does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The app is designed for general audiences and does not contain age-restricted content.

## Third-Party Services

Jono does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or data collection services. The app operates entirely independently.

## AI Features

### On-Device Processing

Jono's AI features, including:

- Voice transcription
- Content transformation (poems, letters, essays)
- Insight generation
- Conversation assistance

All use on-device processing capabilities. When AI features require processing that exceeds on-device capabilities, you will be clearly informed, and processing only occurs with your explicit action.

## Security

Your data's security is ensured by:

- iOS device-level encryption
- No network transmission of personal data
- No external database or server storage
- Sandboxed app storage that prevents other apps from accessing your data

## Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to this Privacy Policy, we will update the "Last Updated" date and notify you through the app. Significant changes will be communicated before they take effect.

## Your Rights

You have the right to:

- Access all your data (it's already on your device)
- Delete any or all of your data
- Export your data (feature availability may vary)
- Use the app without providing any personal information

## Summary

- **100% local storage**: Your data never leaves your device
- **No account needed**: Start using immediately without sign-up
- **No tracking**: We don't monitor or analyze your behavior
- **On-device AI**: Voice and text processing happens locally
- **You're in control**: Delete anything, anytime
- **Uninstall = deletion**: Removing the app removes all your data
