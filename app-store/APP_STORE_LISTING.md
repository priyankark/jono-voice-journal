## Description

Jono is your voice-first journaling companion that turns scattered thoughts into deep personal insights. Speak freely, reflect deeply, and understand yourself better - all while keeping your data completely private on your device.

**YOUR VOICE, YOUR THOUGHTS**
- Record voice notes instantly when inspiration strikes
- Have reflective AI conversations that help you think critically
- Automatic transcription makes every thought searchable

**MEET JONO, YOUR AI COMPANION**
- Tap the glowing orb to start a conversation
- Jono asks thoughtful follow-up questions
- Conversations saved as journal entries

**TRANSFORM YOUR THOUGHTS**
- Turn voice notes into poems, letters, and essays
- Create letters to your future self
- Generate personal affirmations from your experiences

**DISCOVER PATTERNS & INSIGHTS**
- AI-generated insights about your thought patterns
- Track themes and emotional trends over time
- Reflection prompts based on your entries

**PRIVACY BY DESIGN**
- All data stays on your device - always
- No account required, no sign-up
- Voice processing uses Apple's on-device technology

Start your journaling journey today.
