Jono is a world-class, voice-first, privacy focused, totally local, AI enabled and chat-first app that helps humans turn their phones into a device for all those unstructured thoughts and ideas. It's a world-class voice based journaling app that analyzes, structures and visualizes raw thoughts and turns into deep insights so that humans can understand themselves better. 

Technical decisions:
- Use the iphone's core features to the best of your capability
- Apple Intelligence is a key part
- have both an assistant-first as well as GUI first mode where users can record their thoughts
- Assistant mode is a deeper mode because it can ask users follow-ups and collect their inner-most thoughts/ideas, help humans think critically
- Auto/smart tagging
- easily searchable corpus of raw thoughts
- Transformation scenarios - convert thoughts into essays and more
- Insights view to help users understand themselves better
- Generative UX for rich UX experiences
